What a response contains

Success returns {"data": …, "meta": …} with attribution. Errors return {"errors": […]}. Pass exactly one location: city, cityId, or both lat and lon. Add units=us or units=metric.

  • current is a station observation or null. Check observedAt, ageSeconds, and stale; observations older than two hours are stale.
  • forecast has up to 14 periods; hourly has up to 24. NWS forecast text keeps its original units.
  • alertsStatus: "unavailable" means alerts could not be checked. An empty list with alertsStatus: "checked" means none are active; with unavailable, the result is unknown.
  • assembledAt is when the report was built, not when the data were observed. Upstream data are cached for up to 120 seconds.
Coverage: the US and territories that NWS covers. A city forecast is for the city center, and the nearest station may be several kilometers away. This public demo has usage limits and no uptime guarantee.
